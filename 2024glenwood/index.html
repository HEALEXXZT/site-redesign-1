2024 Glenwood Rd. Unit 1

2BR|1.5BA |$2400 per month

A unique space right in the heart of Ditmas Park! Located on beautiful Glenwood Rd, this turn-of-the-19th to 20th-century two-family home built in 1910 offers old-world charm with modern conveniences. This 2 BR/1.5 bath apartment has coffered ceilings, a fireplace, a wrap-around front porch, and beautiful little nooks! The updated kitchen has stainless appliances and laundry tucked away in the pantry. One bedroom has an en-suite half bath. Pets are allowed with approval. Located close to Brooklyn College, residents can use the gym and facilities for a fee with proof of a utility bill. Subway lines nearby can be located at Newkirk Plaza for the Q and B train and the 25 at the Flatbush Junction stop.
