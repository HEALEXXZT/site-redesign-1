420 W.25th St #5B

FLOOR PLAN

VIDEO

LOFT | 1BR | 1BA | CC $1460 | TAXES $1280 | Asking 1.4M

Experience the epitome of modern loft living in the heart of the West Chelsea Art District at Loft 25, a luxurious condominium situated in a former pre-war ink press building. This tranquil yet dramatic loft combines elegant design, upscale finishes, and an ideal location minutes away from the High Line and Hudson Yards.

This bright, airy Zen haven is an exquisitely appointed designer loft with a separate bedroom. It has been featured in two books, Living in Style New York, and Luxury Living New York. A cosmopolitan collection of modern and antique furnishings, including rare Qing Dynasty 18th Century scroll tables (available for purchase) and furnishings by Ted Boerner and Henry Miller – as well as many custom-made fixtures and original works of art – create a sophisticated space that is as diverse as it is harmonious.

A gracious entryway ushers you into the expansive, breathtaking, open-concept living, dining, and entertainment space. The 14’ high ceilings, Nordic ash hardwood floors, and custom ironwork valences elevate the atmosphere. North-facing oversized windows flood the interior with natural light, framing picturesque views. The living area opens into a seamless, sleek, glass Schiffini Italian kitchen - this culinary haven is a work of art! Equipped with top-of-the-line appliances, a refrigerator by Liebherr, a Bosch range and dishwasher, this is the perfect spot for an intimate dinner or large soiree.

The tranquil sleeping space features Individual climate control for year-round comfort. The luxurious bathroom has a soaking tub and oversized shower featuring a rain-head for ultimate relaxation. A custom teak vanity, Italian-made Teknobili fixtures, and imported Porcelanosa ceramic tiles on the floors and walls create the perfect spa-like atmosphere. A roomy custom-fitted Elfa closet offers versatility as a small home office or it is ideal for remote work. The loft features an in-unit washer and dryer. The apartment is pre-wired for high-speed internet, media, and telecom access. A large private storage room transfers with the unit, a luxury in NYC!

Originally erected in 1912, Loft 25 is a converted former industrial masonry ink-press warehouse loft building constructed with a refined state-of-the-art aesthetic characterized by high ceilings and expansive interiors. Loft 25 offers an array of luxury amenities, including a 24-hour doorman, a live-in superintendent, a dramatic lobby leading to a double-height lounge, an intimate garden with a reflecting pool, a fully-equipped fitness center, a screening room, and a spectacular 5,000 sq ft landscaped roof deck with breathtaking river views, an outdoor shower, restrooms, BBQs, and lounge areas. The building is pet-friendly.

Enjoy easy access to the iconic High Line, Chelsea Piers, Hudson Yards, art galleries, world-class dining, shopping, and an exciting nightlife scene. Little Island, an elevated public park along the Hudson River, is just moments away and the C and E subways and cross-town bus options are within a short distance.
