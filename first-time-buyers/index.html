First-time buyer FAQ

FIRST TIME BUYERS

Confidence in your real estate professional is key.  We have over 20 years of experience helping new home buyers forge the path to ownership.  From property selection to negotiations, Caldwellings has you covered.   

PROPERTY SELECTION

We assist you in the buying process by providing you with a full list of properties that match your criteria and then providing objective info on each to help narrow down the choices.

NEGOTIATIONS

We negotiate on your behalf to attain the best terms possible.

ATTORNEY REFERRALS

Once you’ve found a home, we can provide you with attorney referrals if necessary.

DO I PAY FOR MY REAL ESTATE AGENT’S SERVICES?

We require all clients to sign a Buyer’s Broker agreement, as required by law. If you choose to hire us, we’ll discuss all the options.

SHOULD I RENT OR OWN?

Real Estate properties are investments that can be sold later, often at a considerable return. Additionally, owning property can offer significant tax advantages. When you rent, you help to pay off the Landlord's mortgage. Why put your money in someone else’s pocket? Consult your accountant or financial planning professional for more information.

WHAT CAN I AFFORD TO SPEND?

We connect you with mortgage professionals who will walk you through the mortgage process and obtain your pre-approval. What you can afford depends on:

  • gross household income

  • down payment, typically 20% (although could be less depending on loan types)

  • mortgage interest rate

  • current monthly expenses

ARE THERE ANY ADDITIONAL COSTS OR EXPENSES?

Yes, closing costs for the buyer depends upon the type of property purchased.  See our buyer closing costs page under the navigation menu or click here.
