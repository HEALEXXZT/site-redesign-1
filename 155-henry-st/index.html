155 Henry Street #4FG, Brooklyn Heights

2BR/2BA | $1,500,000 | Maint. $1943

Rare and unique 2BR/2BA combined unit in historic Brooklyn Heights! This bright and airy apartment is a perfect combination of classic details and modern living space. The open floor plan feels loft-like and the beamed ten foot (10') ceilings provide pre-war detail. 

A sleek chef's kitchen echos the design of vintage kitchens past, but is brimming with modern amenities including a SubZero refrigerator, a concealed walk-in pantry, built-in storage, a rolling hidden dry goods rack, and a washer/dryer. The windowed kitchen is equipped with a long counter-top bar area, perfect for a quick breakfast or a grand place to serve your guests during a cocktail party (there is even a built in wine rack). Adjacent to the kitchen is an ample dining space…finally you can have that long table you always wanted! Off the kitchen there is a windowed full bathroom. 

As you enter the large yet cozy living room, a wall of windows bathes the space in bright, soft light. Whether you are entertaining or spending some quiet time at home, the living room is the perfect place to relax.

The Master and second bedrooms are beyond the living room, providing privacy from the main area of the apartment. A windowed full bath with walk in shower completes the private quarters. There are ample closets throughout including a linen closet.

Located just a short three block walk from the popular Brooklyn Promenade and playgrounds, this pet friendly, well- maintained co-op is right in the middle of the best of Brooklyn Heights. The building has a bike room and storage (waiting list) in the basement, and a laundry room. It is within walking distance of historic churches, the Court Houses, Trader Joe's, pharmacies, groceries, and of course, many celebrated Brooklyn Heights restaurants and specialty food shops. Downtown Brooklyn and Downtown Manhattan are a short commute. The subway is a quick walk down two tree-lined blocks. 

24 hours notice required to show. Co-Brokers welcomed.
