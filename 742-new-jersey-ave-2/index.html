742 New Jersey Ave. #2

3BR | 2BA | $3500 per month

Welcome home to this newly renovated 3BR/1BA apartment situated on a quiet tree-lined street in Brooklyn! This bright and beautiful 2nd floor apartment is sunny and stylish with exposed brick, wide plank oak wood flooring, and soaring 12ft ,ceilings with teak stained beams. Plentiful windows and a skylight provide constant bright light. The large living room has quiet garden views and includes a Pullman kitchen complete with stainless steel appliances and modern grey cabinetry with interior lighting. The apartment has 9 cedar-lined closets and there is a hidden attic storage space in the smallest bedroom. The primary bedroom has built-in storage, a wall of bowed windows, and exposed brick - a charming restful space. The other two bedrooms also have high ceilings and ample closets.

The building has been renovated fully with new heating , water tanks, electrical, and ac units installed.

This historic building was built at the turn of the 19th to 20th century when this part of Brooklyn was still farmland. Now it is in the middle of bustling Brooklyn with easy access to supermarkets and laundry within a block or two. The 3 (7 min) and L (10 min) trains, numerous bus lines and a 25 min ride to JFK and the beach make transportation convenient. Gateway Center Mall which is just over a mile away has Target, Home Depot, Shoprite and Lidl as well as restaurants and other big box retailers.

Requirements: Credit and background check, first month and security. Vouchers accepted for families with vouchers for the asking rent range, utilities not included.

PLEASE TEXT OR REPLY VIA THE SITE TO MAKE AN APPOINTMENT. DO NOT CALL. APARTMENT MUST BE SEEN BEFORE APPLYING. ALL OCCUPANTS MUST BE PRESENT AT ANY SHOWINGS.
