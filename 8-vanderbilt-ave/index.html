8 Vanderbilt Ave, #7F

FLOORPLAN HERE

1BR | 1BA | $675,000 | CC $438

JUST REDUCED! Welcome to 7F, a bright, spacious, one-bedroom near the Brooklyn Navy Yard. The kitchen includes sparkling Caesarstone countertops, stainless steel appliances (including a dishwasher and microwave). A sizeable spa-like bathroom (complete with deep bathtub) creates a serene place to unwind. The open concept living/dining room can comfortably fit large furniture and accommodate a dining area. Both the bedroom and living room offer cityscape views. The bedroom has a walk-in closet and additional closet space is deep and well thought out.

The building’s roof deck has 360 degree views in addition to a BBQ and landscaping for residents to enjoy. A 30,000 square foot private park with a courtyard is solely for the use of building residents.

Amenities including a smart intercom system, key fob entry, bike room, fitness room, package room, cold storage, and laundry room. There is an on-site superintendent available. The neighborhood offers a variety of entertainment and eating destinations. Fort Greene's farmers market and DUMBO's waterfront are nearby, or stop by Wegman’s for groceries or Brooklyn Roasting Company for coffee. The always bustling Myrtle Avenue is lined with shops and services and is a quick walk away.

Public transportation:

BUS: B57/B69/B62 bus lines only one block away

SUBWAY: F train at York, and the A, C, F &R lines at Jay St-MetroTech, the B, D, Q & N at Dekalb Ave, as well as the G train at Clinton-Washington Ave.

FERRY: to Wall Street/34th Street

24-hour showing notice required. Please note: Caldwellings Real Estate follows required social distancing and mask protocols as required by NYS.
