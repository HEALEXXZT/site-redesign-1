BUSHWICK BROOKLYN LIVE/WORK CALDWELLINGS REAL ESTATE

FLOORPLAN HERE

MIXED-USE LIVE/WORK BUILDING | $1.325M

COMMERCIAL SPACE ALSO AVAILABLE FOR LEASE AT $4000 per month

Want a quick commute? Welcome home to your own private get-away in the heart of Bushwick where you can take a flight to your new office (a flight of stairs)!

The commercial space faces Wilson Ave and is zoned retail/office. The space is approx 1600 square feet and is currently configured as represented in the floor plan but there is a possibility of converting the space to a white box. This is the perfect space for a yoga or pilates studio, architect office, co-working space, start-up, medical office, and more!

The second-floor apartment was renovated in 2019. This 1,000 sq ft bright and spacious two bedroom, one bathroom apartment (with large private roof terrace) is flooded with light! The living room is large enough to have an ample dining area and a full living room.

Like to cook? The huge kitchen features stainless steel appliances (dishwasher, gas stove, and microwave hood combo), plentiful counter space, and cabinet storage. In addition to both a linen and a coat closet, each bedroom has great closet space as well. The renovated bathroom has heated tile floors – perfect for cold winter mornings. 

The smaller bedroom leads out to a large private terrace that can accommodate furniture and plants with plenty of room to spare!


The building is a few blocks from the Myrtle-Wyckoff L and M subway stop and short walks to other amenities.
