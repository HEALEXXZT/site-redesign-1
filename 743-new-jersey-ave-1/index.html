743 New Jersey Ave #1

2BR | 1BA $2700 per month

Situated on the parlor level of a well-kept two family home, this bright and sunny 2BR/1BA is freshly painted and ready for you to to move in! There is ample closet space including a large walk-in closet, kitchen with a stainless steel dishwasher, stove, and a new fridge. Cooking gas, heat, and hot water included. Linden Park is a two block walk away. Laundry is a block away as are many small local restaurants. The 3 and L trains are a 7-10 min walk away and the B15/20/82/83 are nearby on New Lots Ave. It's only a short ride to Broadway Junction and the A/C/J lines there. Bushwick and Crown heights are a 20 minute train ride away. Close to the Gateway shopping center with Pathmark, Target, Lidl, Home Depot and a variety of clothing stores and restaurants. Centrally located near Pennsylvania Ave, the Belt Parkway and Linden Blvd . First month and security, credit and background checks required.
