156 Pulaski #1C

$1.275M | CC $1092 | Taxes $284 per month

Floorplan

Brooklyn Condo with Townhouse Charm

This stunning, sun-drenched duplex is nestled in a boutique, self-managed building with exceptionally low monthly carrying charges. This unique pre-war condo spans 1,159 square feet and features 2-bedrooms plus home office and 2.5 bathrooms. The open floor plan provides an ideal live-work-entertain canvas - it's condo living with a townhouse feel!

The Layout:

Dramatic 11-foot ceilings instantly elevate the main living level. Sun-Drenched Rooms with oversized windows paired with multiple exposures flood the residence with all-day sunlight. Designed with an open-concept flow, the main floor integrates a sunny, well-appointed kitchen with exposed brick, a spacious dining area, and a large living room - a dream entertaining space! This floor includes 1.5 bathrooms and one bedroom overlooking the garden.
The lower level is accessed by a beautiful spiral staircase. This highly flexible lower floor offers a stylish full bathroom and bedroom with a separate room ( walk-in closet, office, art studio, for example) and provides a quiet, private retreat separate from the main living space.
Step outside into your own private decked oasis with hydrangeas and climbing wisteria that bloom beautifully season after season. It is the perfect backdrop for morning coffee, alfresco dining, or evening relaxation and entertaining.

The Neighborhood

Tucked onto a tree-lined residential block in Bedford-Stuyvesant, this home sits just moments from the Myrtle Avenue G train. Enjoy immediate access to the vibrant dining, parks, local cafes, and rich culture crossing between Bed-Stuy and Clinton Hill.
