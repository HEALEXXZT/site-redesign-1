Buyer services

SEAMLESS SERVICE FROM CONTRACT TO CLOSING

Whether you’re a first time buyer, relocating, up-sizing or down-sizing, we guide you step-by-step through the buying process and ensure that everything flows together smoothly from contract to closing

BUYING IN SIX EASY STEPS:

  • STEP ONE:  Select your purchasing team.  Find an agent, mortgage professional and attorney to help you through the process. 
  • STEP TWO: Get pre-qualified for a mortgage.  Discuss your “wish list” with your agent.
  • STEP THREE: Start looking! Through weekday/evening appointments, internet browsing and open houses, we’ll find you the perfect property.  
  • STEP FOUR: Let’s make a deal!  Once you are ready to make an offer, we’ll put together a professional proposal and submit it for consideration.  Once accepted, contact your attorney and mortgage professionals to get the due diligence, contracts and financing started.    
  • STEP FIVE:  Both parties sign the contract.
  • CO-OP/CONDO:  If there is an application , we’ll do the paperwork, submit it to the managing agent and await board approval. 
  • SINGLE FAMILY OR MULTI-FAMILY DWELLING:  Now is the time to call in property inspectors.  Most contracts are contingent upon inspection and allow an out should something costly be discovered.    
  • STEP SIX: Let’s close!  Once approved, the attorneys, bank, and management company (co-op/condo only) confer to schedule a closing date.  At closing, the final paperwork is signed, checks are submitted and keys are exchanged.  Congratulations! You are now a homeowner.
