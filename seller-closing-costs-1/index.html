Seller closing costs

SELLER CLOSING COSTS

 

CONDO 

  • Attorney: Consult your attorney

  • Broker: Typically 3-6% (negotiable)

  • Processing Fee: $450+

  • NY State Transfer Tax: $4 per $1,000 of price

  • NYS Equalization Fee: $75

  • NYC Transfer Tax : SEE BELOW

  • Commercial: Up to $500,000 = 1.425% or $500,000+ = 2.625%

ADDITIONAL REAL ESTATE EXPENSES

  • Non Deed transfers (ie co-ops): $50

  • Residential Deed Transfers : $75

  • Commercial Deed Transfers: $165

  • UCC-3 Filing Fee: $100

  • Miscellaneous Condominium Charges: Vary by building

  • Pick-up/Payoff Fee: $250-$500

  • Note: For condominiums in new developments, the Purchaser will often costs normally paid by the Seller. These include Seller attorney fees as well as NY and NYC Transfer Taxes.

 

 

 

CO-OP

  • Attorney: Consult your attorney

  • Broker: Typically 3-6% (negotiable)

  • Co-op Attorney: $450+

  • Flip Tax: Typically 1% to 3% of price (if applicable)

  • Stock Transfer Tax: $0.05 per shar

  • Move-out Deposit/Fee: Varies by buildin

  • NY State Transfer Tax: $4 per $1,000 of price

  • NYS Equalization Fee: $75

  • NYC Transfer Tax : SEE BELOW

  • Commercial: Up to $500,000 = 1.425% or $500,000+ = 2.625%

ADDITIONAL REAL ESTATE EXPENSES

  • Non Deed transfers (ie co-ops): $50

  • Residential Deed Transfers : $75

  • Commercial Deed Transfers: $165

  • UCC-3 Filing Fee: $100

  • Miscellaneous Co-op Charges: Vary by building

  • Pick-up/Payoff Fee: $250-$500.

 

TOWNHOME/SINGLE FAMILY

  • Attorney: Consult your attorney

  • Broker: Typically 3- 6% (negotiable)

  • NY State Transfer Tax: $4 per $1,000 of price

  • NYS Equalization Fee: $75

  • NYC Transfer Tax : SEE BELOW

  • Commercial: Up to $500,000 = 1.425% or $500,000+ = 2.625%

ADDITIONAL REAL ESTATE EXPENSES

  • Non-Deed Transfers = $50

  • Residential Deed Transfers= $75

  • Commercial Deed Transfers = $165

  • Miscellaneous Title Fees: $200-$500

  • Pick-up / Payoff Fee to Title Closer: $100-$300

NYC TRANSFER TAXES FOR ALL RESIDENTIAL PROPERTIES
