203 West 90th #4F

$4700 Per Month

Welcome home to this massive 1BR (convertible 2)!  Situated right in the heart of the UWS, this spacious and lovely apartment has windows in every room!  

The large chef's kitchen, complete with a dishwasher, is perfect for whipping up a gourmet meal for your guests.  The living area has the potential to carve out a sizable office space or study while still having plenty of room for dining and relaxing in the living room. 

Closets abound in this apartment! The bedroom has a wall of custom closets complete with a locking drawer for your valuables. There are three other deep closets - two in the hallway and one in the living room.

The bedroom has two windows and gets good light, and the windowed bathroom is renovated and relaxing. 

The building was built in 1928, with a modern addition in 2005.  It is close to the express trains at 96th Street, one block to Broadway, and walking distance to Central and Riverside Parks, where you can enjoy tennis or go for a run.  Enjoy all the restaurants and activities the UWS has to offer!  It is also a short commute to Columbia University.

Doorman building, fitness center, laundry in the building and the unit. Board approval required.
