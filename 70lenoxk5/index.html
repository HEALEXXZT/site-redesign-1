70 Lenox Rd, #5K

SOLD - 70 Lenox Road, #K5 Brooklyn, NY

$425,000 | Maintenance $761.92

Sunny and spacious south facing co-op home in Flatbush, Brooklyn.  This charming one bedroom one bath features approximately 1200 square feet of living space.  The large entry foyer opens to a living room that is big enough to accomodate an office space. The kitchen is eat-in and has a dishwasher.  The hallway to the bedroom and bathroom  houses three  large closets and the bathroom has it's own separate linen/utility closet. The massive bedroom is large enough to accomodate a king-sized bed with room to spare for other furniture.  Art deco details abound! Elevator building with live-in super.
