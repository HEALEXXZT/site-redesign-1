420 West 25th Street

FURNISHED ONLY Bright and airy Zen haven in Manhattan. This exquisitely appointed designer loft with separate bedroom has been featured in two books, Living in Style New York, and Luxury Living New York.

A cosmopolitan collection of modern and antique furnishings, including rare Qing Dynasty 18th Century scroll tables and furnishings by Ted Boerner and Henry Miller (as well as many custom made fixtures and original works of art) create a sophisticated space that is as diverse as it is harmonious.

The open, sleek, tempered glass Schiffini Kitchen with Bosch and Liebherr appliances make entertaining stylish and simple. Retreat to your lush Duravit bathroom where a full soaking tub awaits. Ample closet space, 12' ceilings and 8' windows help you breathe freely. W/D, cable/phone/internet and utilities included.

Loft 25 is a 24hr doorman building featuring a 5,000 sq ft roof terrace with views of the Hudson River, a rear courtyard with reflecting pool, well-equipped gym and a screening room. Apartment available for 3 or 6 month lease but long term preferred.

RENT:$6000 per month
