904 Lafayette #1

904 Lafayette #1 | 2BR 1BA | Backyard access | $3350

Floorplan

Bright garden-level two-bedroom in the heart of Bedford-Stuyvesant!

The living room/kitchen is built for relaxing and entertaining, and a whole-house audio system lets you fill every room with music. The galley kitchen features stainless steel appliances with dishwasher. A modern full bath provides a deep tub for soaking after a long day. Both bedrooms overlook the backyard for quiet green views, and one steps right out to the yard. You’ll enjoy coffee out on your patio and have access to a portion of the yard. The apartment has central heat and AC as well as a washer dryer. There is a bike rack in the entry foyer. Sorry, no pets

Features

2 bed / 1 bath, garden level

Backyard access from one bedroom

Reading nook

In-unit washer/dryer

Dishwasher

Bike rack

The B38 is steps away and JMZ trains are nearby.
