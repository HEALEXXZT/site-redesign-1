748 New Jersey Ave #2

Newly renovated 3 bedroom apartment.  Includes radiant floor heating, stainless steel appliances (including dishwasher) and gorgeous light via skylight and many windows.
Farmers markets within walking distance on Wednesdays and Saturdays.  There is also “renew lots” arts space right down the street where you can find art studios, small eateries and pop-up shops all housed in shipping containers. The 3 train (Pennsylvania Ave stop) is a 7 minute walk to the platform and the L train (at Livonia) is 10 minutes.  Local library, post office also a short walk. Grocery stores, laundry and pharmacy within a block of the house.  New Gateway Shopping Center nearby. Tenant pays heat, hot water and electric. Radiant floor heating is a low cost heating method.  Guarantors accepted.  Must have decent credit. 1st, last and security. NO FEE
