425 Lincoln Pl 1L

1BR | 1BA | $390,000 | $433 maintenance

Why rent when you can own? This cozy pre-war one-bedroom one-bath apartment has stainless steel appliances, plentiful light, good closet space, a windowed bathroom, and a washer-dryer hookup. There is even a storage locker in the basement that transfers with the unit. And all for a low monthly maintenance of $433!

This Crown Heights gem is steps away from Brooklyn Museum, Brooklyn Botanic Garden, Prospect Park, and the 2/3/4/5 subway lines at Brooklyn Museum and Franklin Avenue. The neighborhood has abundant restaurants, the Grand Army Plaza farmer's market, and many community events.

425 Lincoln Place is a three-story, nine-unit, well-maintained building built in 1907. The building is pet-friendly (dogs upon approval).
